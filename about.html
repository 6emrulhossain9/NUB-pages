Our story

A university founded on possibility.

Northern University Bangladesh was established on 17 October 2002 by a group of academics, with sponsorship from the International Business Agriculture & Technology (IBAT) Trust, now known as NUB Trust.

NUB is based in Dhaka, with its permanent campus near Hajj Camp in Ashkona. Its academic structure brings together five faculties and programs across professional, scientific, business, humanities, legal and health fields.
