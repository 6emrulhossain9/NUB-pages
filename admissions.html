Admission requirements

Know your starting point.

The summary below follows NUB’s published general admission information. Program-specific requirements may differ, so confirm the latest criteria before applying.

01

Bachelor’s programs

NUB’s published general requirement is a minimum GPA of 2.5 in both SSC and HSC or equivalent examinations. The page also lists O Level in five subjects and A Level in two subjects with a minimum C grade in each, or a US High School Diploma.

02

Postgraduate programs

A relevant bachelor’s degree is the general entry requirement listed by NUB. Check the specific faculty page for the program you are interested in.

i

Please verify before applying. Admission rules, deadlines, documents and fees can change. Use the official NUB admission page or contact the Admission Office for the latest details.
